PickPilot is a sports research site. Phase 1 is NBA only, two tabs: Games and Players. A person looks up games, lines, and players before they bet. It is not a sportsbook. Nobody places a bet inside it. They research, then the odds can be saved to a slip.

The current Games screen, which Gemini must not copy:

- Dark, quiet, research-tool look.
- A left rail for navigation, a main column, and a right-hand panel called Stand Outs.
- The main column is titled "Upcoming Games".
- Date tabs across the top.
- Column headings: Match, Spread, Moneyline, Total.
- One rounded card per game. Each card has two team rows. Each row has a logo, the team name, the record, and three odds cells.
- The best price is marked with a small green dot.
- Clicking a card opens the game. Clicking an odds cell adds it to a research slip.
